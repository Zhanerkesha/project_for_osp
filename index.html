Project Timeline

Our project is divided into five main phases, from initial planning to final presentation.

  1. Phase 1: Research & Planning

    Identify project requirements, research ideas, and assign team roles.

  2. Phase 2: Design

    Plan the website structure, layout, and content.

  3. Phase 3: Development

    Build the website and integrate individual team contributions.

  4. Phase 4: Testing & Review

    Test website functionality, review content, and fix errors.

  5. Phase 5: Final Presentation

    Present the completed website and demonstrate its features.
